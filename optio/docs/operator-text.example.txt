Custos and the task service serve the same Foxhole guild. Each tool reads the guild of the player.

Custos holds the stockpiles, the items in them, and the structures that hold them. The task service
holds the tasks that players ask the guild to do, and the players who do them.

A label is the name of a structure in Custos. A status is the state of a task in the task service.

Ask Custos about the quantity or the place of an item, and about a structure. Ask the task service
about a task and about who does it. A question about the supplies of a task can need both: get the
task first, then the items from Custos.
